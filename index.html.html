Kedarnath

Kedarnath is located in Uttarakhand, India. It is one of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. The temple is surrounded by the beautiful Himalayas. It is a famous Hindu pilgrimage destination..

Trimbakeshwar

Trimbakeshwar Temple is located near Nashik, Maharashtra. It is one of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. The temple is famous for its religious importance. The Godavari River originates near Trimbak.

Grishneshwar

Grishneshwar Temple is located near Ellora in Maharashtra. It is one of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. The temple is famous for its beautiful architecture. It is an important Hindu pilgrimage destination.

Omkareshwar

Omkareshwar is located in Madhya Pradesh, India. It is one of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. The temple is situated on an island in the Narmada River. It is a popular Hindu pilgrimage destination.
